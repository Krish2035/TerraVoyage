TerraVoyage — World Country & Tourism Specifications Explorer

Welcome to TerraVoyage, deployed on Vercel. An interactive global atlas and tourism intelligence platform providing verified specifications for all 195 sovereign nations across Europe, Asia, Americas, Africa, and Oceania.

Global Tourism Intelligence & Explorer Features

  • 195 Sovereign Nations: Capital cities, populations, GDP per capita, currencies, driving sides, electrical plugs, and official languages.
  • Premier Tourist Attractions & 3D Landmarks: In-depth specifications, architectural heritage, ticket benchmarks, Google Maps integration, and live Street View 360 panoramas.
  • Interactive World Map: Vector equirectangular geographic radar with real-time country coordinates, pan and zoom.
  • Live Weather & Peak Travel Seasons: Current temperature, atmospheric conditions, and climate-aware visitation recommendations.
  • Instant Currency Converter & Daily Budget Meter: Real-time FX conversion and backpacker, mid-tier, and luxury cost of living estimates.
  • Smart Visa & Passport Checker: Entry requirements, maximum stay allowances, and mandatory travel documents.
  • Local Phrasebook with Native Audio: Native scripts, phonetic pronunciation guides, and authentic speech synthesis.
  • Custom Travel Itinerary Builder: Drag-and-drop schedule planner, exportable travel summaries, and offline sync.
